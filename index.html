How an engagement runs

From first call to handoff in six steps

You always know what happens next, what it costs, and what done looks like.

  1. Consultation

    30 minutes, free. Your environment, goals, deadline and constraints.

  2. Assessment

    Read-only access. We review your configuration and interview IT and a few users.

  3. Recommendation

    Findings, a roadmap, a fixed price and named success criteria.

  4. Design & pilot

    Target design built in a test group, then piloted with IT and 5–10 users.

  5. Guided rollout

    Department waves with your team alongside, user comms and a rollback plan.

  6. Knowledge transfer

    As-built docs, runbooks, recorded walkthroughs and 2 weeks of follow-up.

FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from hiring an MSP?

An MSP supports your IT every month and is usually paid per user or device. We're brought in for specific projects, paid a fixed fee for each, and finish by handing everything to your team or your MSP. We don't sell licenses or tools, so our advice isn't shaped by what we resell.

Do you do the hands-on work, or just write recommendations?

Both. We design the solution and build it alongside your team in your tenant. The difference is that the engagement ends, and your people know how it works when it does.

We already have an MSP. Will this cause friction?

Usually the opposite. We don't compete for ongoing support, so MSPs often bring us in themselves. They keep day-to-day work, we lead the project, and they inherit a documented environment.

Our Macs aren't managed at all. Where do we start?

We set up Jamf Pro and Apple Business Manager so new and existing Macs enroll automatically, then apply encryption, patching and security settings. Next we connect Jamf to Entra ID, so Mac users sign in with their Microsoft account and only compliant Macs reach company apps. If you're also moving Windows devices from hybrid join to the cloud, the Macs follow the same plan and timeline.

We're just starting our business. Is this for us?

Yes. The New Business IT Launch is built for companies of 1 to 50 people that haven't set up IT yet. We recommend what to buy and what to skip, whether you run on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and give you the one-time and monthly cost of each item. We can also set it all up remotely so it's ready on your first day. We don't resell hardware or licenses, so the recommendations are based only on what your business needs.

Do we need to replace our laptops?

Usually not. Most Windows 10 and 11 devices can move from hybrid join to Entra-only in place. The assessment flags any hardware that can't.

Will staff be offline during the move?

No. We migrate in waves, one department at a time, after a pilot with IT and a few volunteers. Users hear from us a week and a day before their wave, and every wave has a rollback plan.

Do you work on-site or remotely?

Most work is remote, so we take clients anywhere in the US. We're based in Greater Boston for workshops or kickoffs in person.

What happens after the project ends?

You get an as-built design, an admin runbook, recorded walkthroughs and two weeks of follow-up. After that your team runs it. If you'd like a senior second opinion on call, the Fractional Endpoint Architect retainer covers that.
